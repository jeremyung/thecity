# Dense Tokyo style comparisons

Generated with the built-in image generation tool from the five user-supplied
photos (images 4 and 5 show the same railway view), using locked concepts 04 and
01 as style references. Supplemental studies; these do not replace the locked
concepts and are not runtime screenshots or texture assets.

Each board: **Last Train, First Light** on the left, **Little Tokyo** on the right.

- [Neighborhood mosaic](01-neighborhood-mosaic.png)

- [Neighborhood intersection](05-neighborhood-intersection.png) — added from the subsequent photo set; prompt in `intersection-prompt.json`. The other three photos repeat scenes above.

Look for packed small lots, mixed building heights, recognizable roof and facade
rhythms, infrastructure integrated into urban fabric, and selective lens focus.
The generated scenes are interpretive. Camera crop and geometry drift between
panels, especially the river scene, so these are qualitative artistic references,
not a controlled same-geometry renderer comparison. Blur is deliberately more
visible in Little Tokyo, but runtime style distinction must also hold with DOF off.

Prompts and generation provenance are recorded alongside the boards. Source
photos were supplied by the user; stock-watermarked references are not claimed
as project-owned photography. No runtime asset inclusion or license inference.
