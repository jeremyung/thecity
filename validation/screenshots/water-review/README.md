# Rejected water experiment — 2026-10-10

- `before-miniature-16.jpg`: baseline Miniature water, lighting preview 16:00.
- `rejected-miniature-16.jpg`: experimental physical water material (IOR 1.333),
  view-angle reflection blending, and greater roughness, at the same preview hour.

Both show the 850-building mature runtime fixture. The camera poses are not
pixel-matched: the baseline was frozen during an eased skyline transition and
the trial was captured after settling. These captures cannot establish a
controlled A/B improvement or performance comparison. The broad pale river
reflection remained visually distracting; the experiment was reverted rather
than recorded as an accepted artistic improvement. Current water remains the
committed implementation with animated waves, rain ripples and planar reflections.
