## Changes against the supplied crossing reference

- Four product-led fictional campaigns (cider, records, travel, coffee), each with two art-directed scenes, sharp Japanese/English copy, an illustrated hero, and timed transitions. Type is kept away from the least visible corner-return area. One 1024×1024 atlas is shared by all campaign/style pairs, approximately 5.33 MiB RGBA8 including mipmaps. Cabinet seams, a restrained highlight and exposure-safe modulation support the artwork. These are illustrated advertisements, not decoded photoreal video.
- Transparent physical podium glazing reveals shop displays, café seating and bookshelves. A separate entrance leads to escalator silhouettes through a matching floor opening; canopy, frame, steps and threshold align. Small storefront props, recessed screen housings, deeper mullions, louvres and rooftop equipment add construction detail. Cladding has small nonperiodic finish variation and restrained base staining rather than repeated floor-wide grime bands.
- Two fixed non-shadowing spotlights approximate media spill, selected from nearby visible, active, allocated landmark screens. They follow campaign palette and presentation time, and turn off by day, in static/off modes, for inactive businesses and far LOD. The pool is reused/reset/disposed with the renderer. This is an approximation, not area-light transport or true emissive GI.
- A reusable nine-building, paved commercial corner uses the existing road, sidewalk, scramble-crossing, tree, pedestrian and vehicle rendering. Review actors are supported frozen poses, not a new traffic simulation. Actual cities keep engine-owned movement. The station-area crossing is generated by the production crossing function and tested. This fixture does not change engine parcel growth or street planning.
