## Design Context

### Users
People seeking a relaxing browser-based living diorama to watch and explore. Their primary activity is observing a city develop, following people and places, and adjusting the camera and time.

### Brand Personality
Miniature, observant, alive. Evoke calm curiosity and attachment to ordinary city life.

### Aesthetic Direction
Tokyo-inspired tilt-shift photography: elevated views, selective focus, tiny everyday activity, waterfronts, crossings, transit, and warm night lighting. The supplied photographic reference establishes the toy-like appearance. Each new city differs, while its internal development has coherent causes and history.

### Design Principles
1. Let the city occupy the screen; reveal controls and explanations when needed.
2. Make growth and daily activity causally connected and legible.
3. Preserve recognizable places as their surroundings evolve.
4. Use miniature scale and selective focus without obscuring the activity being followed.
5. Prioritize relaxing observation over management, scoring, and compulsory tasks.

### Implementation Direction
The experience is a living watchable simulation with no management or game loop. Last Train, First Light is the first renderer; Little Tokyo is the preferred visual direction and second selectable renderer. Both use one shared 3D world with dynamic water, full-scene anti-aliasing, daily lighting, and spatial city sound beneath an indie lo-fi score. Rust compiled to WASM runs the authoritative simulation in a worker.

The finite city expands through visible road and rail construction, then continues through infill and renewal. A gentle camera reveals connected growth and moves between readable scenes. The implementation specification index is `specs/README.md`; numeric budgets remain provisional until measured.

### Quality commitment

The user explicitly prioritizes quality and beauty. Luna delegation reduces implementation cost, not the acceptance standard. The primary agent owns comparisons with the concept boards and must reject visually generic results even when functional tests pass. Preserve composition, street detail, material character, water, and stable anti-aliased edges. Measure performance and reduce optional distant effects before degrading the focal scene. The application remains unfinished until the visual release gate in `specs/08-delivery.md` is satisfied.
